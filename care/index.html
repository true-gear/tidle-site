Care and storage.

Look after it and it'll look after you for a long time.

Set up your dockCare and storageShare your lake day

After every trip

  • Rinse it with fresh water, especially after salt or muddy water.
  • Let it dry fully before you roll it up, so nothing goes mouldy in the bag.
  • Check the valve cap is screwed down and clean.

In the water for the season

  • Keep it tied off with a little slack, and check the ropes after wind.
  • Top the pressure up if it softens in cool weather. Air shrinks overnight.
  • Take it out ahead of big storms, floods or rising water.

Storing it

  • Store it rolled in its bag, out of direct sun, away from sharp edges and rodents.
  • Don't store it wet, and don't leave it fully pumped up in hot sun on land for long.

Small punctures

Clean and dry the area, cut a patch from the repair kit with rounded corners, apply the glue to both surfaces as the kit describes, press it down firmly and let it cure before you pump it up again. For anything bigger than a small nick, or anything at a seam or the valve, contact us under the warranty.
