Where it works

Built for the bank, not the marina.

Muddy shores, gravel ramps, farm dams and slow rivers. Somewhere dry to step on and off, load the boat and tie up while you're ashore.

The build

Made to take a beating at the ramp.

Non-slip traction pads

On the Grain edition, sand-coloured pads run along the deck where your feet land. The kids will find out first.

20 cm drop stitch

Thousands of fine threads tie the top to the bottom, so the deck stays flat and firm instead of bulging like a pool toy.

D-rings and handles

Stainless D-rings to tie the boat off, and webbing handles to drag it up the bank.

One valve, one pump

Pump it up at the bank and tie off. The Harbour Y comes with an electric pump to do the work.

Packs into one bag

Let it down, roll it up and it goes in the carry bag, beside the esky in the ute tray.

Repair kit in the box

Patches and glue for the bank, so a small nick doesn't end the weekend.
