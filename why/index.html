Why Tidle

Why choose a Tidle?

We sold other brands' docks before we built our own. These are the five things we made sure of.

Grip

A deck that doesn't turn slippery.

Most inflatable docks are slick PVC the moment they get wet. The Grain edition has non-slip traction pads right where you step off the bow, and the kids are the first to notice.

The build

Firm under your feet.

20cm of drop stitch holds the deck flat and level, so it doesn't sag or bounce when you step on with the gear. Stainless D-rings and webbing handles where you need them.

3year warranty

Warranty

Three years, and a local business behind it.

Every Tidle carries a three-year warranty. Claims go to True Gear in Australia, not back through a distributor or an overseas inbox.

People

Call and a real person picks up.

The team at True Gear sells and supports docks every day. Ring 03 7056 6732 or text 0476 330 590 and you'll talk to someone who knows them inside out.

Honest advice

We'll talk you out of the wrong dock.

Tell us your boat and your bank. If the smaller, cheaper dock does the job, that's the one we'll recommend.

Still deciding?

Tell us about your boat. We'll tell you which one.

What you run, where you launch, and how many are on board. We'll pick the dock we'd buy in your shoes, and if the Launch 3 will do the job, we'll say so.
