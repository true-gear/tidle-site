Our story

We sold other people's docks first.

Tidle comes from True Gear, an Australian outdoor store. This is why we built our own.

True Gear sells gear for people who spend their weekends outdoors: power, camping, caravanning and boating. Inflatable docks have been part of that range for years, from several of the well-known brands.

Selling them, we heard the same things from owners again and again. The deck gets slippery when it's wet. Some of them flex and bounce more than you'd like with a grown man and an esky on board. And when something goes wrong, the warranty can turn into a long wait on someone overseas.

So we built the dock we'd been trying to find for our customers. Non-slip where your feet land, 20cm of drop stitch, a three-year warranty, and a local team that picks up the phone. That's Tidle.
