Setting up

Unroll, pump, tie off.

No tools and nothing to bolt together. Lay it out on clear ground near the water, pump it up with the electric pump in the box, slide it in and tie off to the D-rings.

Questions before you buy

How long does it take to set up?

Unroll it, pump it up, tie it off. The Harbour Y comes with an electric pump; the Launch docks come with a hand pump.

Can I leave it in the water?

Yes, tied off properly, for the trip or the season. Rinse it, dry it and roll it up when you head home, and store it out of direct sun.

What if it gets a puncture?

Every Tidle comes with a repair kit for small holes on the bank. Anything bigger, contact True Gear under the three-year warranty.
